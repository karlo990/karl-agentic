DO

Keep the cursor tip touching the seal. That contact is the "click".

DON'T

Rotate, tilt or flip the mark. The cursor always points up-left.

DON'T

Swap the colours. The seal is always blue, the cursor always pink.

DON'T

Stretch, squash or add shadows and outlines to the logo.

Colour

Verified Blue leads. Click Pink acts.

Blue carries trust and fills most surfaces. Pink is the action colour: buttons, cursors, highlights and anything a person should click. Use pink sparingly so it always means "do this".

Voice

Speak like a sharp operator, not a robot.

Clients are business owners, not engineers. Talk about hours saved, bookings made and leads followed up. Skip the AI jargon.

Confident
"Your agent books the table."

Not: "Our AI-powered solution may help optimise reservations."

Clear
"Every lead gets a reply in 60 seconds."

Not: "Leveraging LLM orchestration for omnichannel engagement."

Accountable
"You approve it before it goes live."

Trust is the seal. Always show the client stays in control.
