Pro Automation recipes

Every automation has the same shape: a trigger, a brain, and an action. Here are six recipes you can build this week.

WhatsApp booking assistant

01 · TRIGGER → BRAIN → ACTION

New WhatsApp message → AI reads the dates, guests and budget → checks the calendar, holds the room and replies with a confirmation.

TRY THISStart simple: AI drafts the reply, a person taps send.

Instagram keyword DM

Someone comments 'AGENT' on a post → they get an automatic DM with your demo link.

Instant lead follow-up

03 · SALES

New website form → add to CRM → AI writes a personal reply in under a minute → you get a WhatsApp alert.

TRY THISMeasure how fast you reply today, then try to get it under 5 minutes.

Invoice reminders

04 · OPERATIONS

Invoice 3 days overdue → friendly reminder → 7 days → firmer reminder with payment link → 14 days → alert to you.

TRY THISWrite the three messages first; the automation is the easy part.

Monday morning report

05 · REPORTING

Every Monday 7:00 → pull last week's bookings, sales and posts → AI writes a 5-line summary → email to you.

TRY THISList the 5 numbers you actually care about each week.

Review replies

06 · REPUTATION

New Google review → AI drafts a reply in your brand voice → you approve it → it's posted.

TRY THISCollect 3 of your best past replies as examples for the AI.
