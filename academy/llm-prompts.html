Copy these LLM prompts

Works in Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Every prompt follows the formula Role + Task + Context + Format. Replace the [brackets] and copy.

PART 18 PROMPTSFROM OUR INSTAGRAM SERIES

Booking reply

PROMPT 01
You are the friendly booking assistant for [business name], a [type of business] in [town].
Reply to this customer message: "[paste message]".
Available dates: [dates]. Price: [price].
Write a warm WhatsApp reply under 50 words, offer two options and end with a question.

Instagram caption

PROMPT 02
You are a social media manager for [business].
Write 3 Instagram captions for a post about [topic].
Our audience: [who]. Our tone: [e.g. confident, warm, no jargon].
Each caption: a hook in the first line, 2 short lines of value, a call to action, and 5 relevant hashtags.

A month of content

PROMPT 03
You are a content strategist.
Create a 4-week Instagram plan for [business] that sells [product/service] to [audience].
Use 3 content pillars: education, proof, behind the scenes.
Return a table: week, day, format (Reel/carousel/story), hook, call to action.

Summarise reviews

PROMPT 04
Here are our latest customer reviews: [paste reviews].
List the top 3 things customers love and the top 3 complaints, with one quote for each.
Then suggest 3 changes we could make this month. Keep it under 200 words.

Follow-up email

PROMPT 05
You are a polite, confident salesperson for [business].
Write a follow-up email to [name], who asked about [service] [X] days ago and hasn't replied.
Mention one benefit relevant to them: [benefit]. Under 90 words. One clear next step.

FAQ from your website

PROMPT 06
Read this text from our website: [paste text].
Write the 10 questions customers are most likely to ask, with short, accurate answers using only this text.
If the text doesn't answer something important, list it separately as 'Missing info'.

Price increase message

PROMPT 07
You are the owner of [business].
Write a short, honest message telling regular customers our price for [service] is going from [old] to [new] on [date].
Thank them, give one reason, and offer [small perk] to anyone who books before the change.

Turn notes into a proposal

PROMPT 08
Here are my call notes: [paste notes].
Write a one-page proposal for [client] with: their goal, our solution in 3 steps, timeline, what we need from them, and price: [price].
Use plain language and short headings.
