Selected Design skills

Good design follows rules you can learn. Check every post against these before you publish.

PART 17 LESSONSFROM OUR INSTAGRAM SERIES

Hierarchy

01 · RULE 1

Decide the one thing people should see first and make it the biggest. Then the second thing. Everything else is small.

TRY THISSquint at your design. Whatever you still see is your hierarchy.

Two fonts maximum

02 · RULE 2

One display font for headlines and one calm font for body text. Our brand uses Unbounded and Manrope.

TRY THISRebuild an old post using only two fonts and compare.

60 · 30 · 10 colour

03 · RULE 3

60% main colour, 30% secondary, 10% accent. Our accent is pink, so pink means 'click here'.

TRY THISCount the colours in your last five posts. Cut the extras.

White space

04 · RULE 4

Empty space is not wasted. It makes the important thing louder and the design look expensive.

TRY THISDouble the margin around your headline and see how it changes.

Align to a grid

05 · RULE 5

Every edge should line up with something. Use guides in Canva, Figma or Photoshop.

TRY THISTurn on rulers and snap every element to the same left edge.

Contrast you can read

06 · RULE 6

Text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background to be readable (WCAG guidance).

TRY THISCheck your colours with a free contrast checker.

Export at the right size

07 · RULE 7

Instagram feed portrait 1080 × 1350, Stories and Reels 1080 × 1920, square 1080 × 1080.

TRY THISSet up these three sizes as templates in your design app.
