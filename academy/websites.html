ACADEMY / WEB

Launch Websites that book

A website's job is to turn a visitor into a booking. This is the checklist we use for every client site.

IN ONE WEEKEND7 LESSONSFROM OUR INSTAGRAM SERIES

Domain

01 · SATURDAY MORNING

Buy yourbusiness.com (or a local domain) from a registrar such as Cloudflare or Namecheap. Keep it short and easy to say.

TRY THISSay the domain out loud to a friend. If they can't type it, change it.

Builder

02 · SATURDAY

Framer or Webflow for no-code; Next.js or plain HTML for full control. This site is plain HTML on Vercel.

TRY THISPick one and build only the home page first.

One clear action

03 · THE RULE

Every page needs one main button: Book now, WhatsApp us, or Get a quote. Make it pink (your action colour).

TRY THISRemove every button that isn't the main action from your hero.

WhatsApp button

04 · CONVERSION

A wa.me link opens a chat with a pre-filled message. Most local customers prefer it to forms.

TRY THIS
https://wa.me/[country code][number]?text=Hi%2C%20I%27d%20like%20to%20book
Learn more ↗

Host and HTTPS

05 · SUNDAY

Vercel or Netlify host for free on small sites and add HTTPS automatically. Connect your domain in the dashboard.

TRY THISDeploy, then open the site on your phone over mobile data.

Get found

06 · SUNDAY AFTERNOON

Create a Google Business Profile, write a clear page title and description, and add real photos.

TRY THISSearch your business name on Google Maps and fix what you see.
Learn more ↗

Speed check

07 · BEFORE LAUNCH

Compress images (this site uses JPGs under 200 KB) and test with PageSpeed Insights.

TRY THISAim for a mobile score in the green.
