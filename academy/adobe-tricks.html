Useful Adobe tricks

Small Adobe features that save hours every week. If you don't have Creative Cloud, the free alternatives are listed at the end.

FOR CREATORS6 LESSONSFROM OUR INSTAGRAM SERIES

Generative Fill

01 · PHOTOSHOP

Select an area, type what you want (or leave it blank to remove), and Photoshop fills it to match the photo.

TRY THISExtend a square product photo to 4:5 for Instagram with Crop + Generative Expand.
Learn more ↗

Remove Background

02 · PHOTOSHOP

In the Properties panel, Quick Actions → Remove Background cuts out a person or product in one click.

TRY THISCut out a product and place it on a brand-pink background.
Learn more ↗

Image Trace

03 · ILLUSTRATOR

Turns a PNG or JPG logo into sharp vectors, so you can print it on anything at any size.

TRY THISTrace an old logo, then use Expand to edit the shapes.
Learn more ↗

Recolor Artwork

04 · ILLUSTRATOR

Swaps every colour in an illustration to your brand palette at once.

TRY THISRecolour an icon set to Navy, Blue and Pink in one step.
Learn more ↗

Auto captions

05 · PREMIERE PRO

Speech to Text transcribes your video and creates captions you can style. Most Reels are watched on mute, so captions matter.

TRY THISCaption a 30-second talking-head Reel and style it in your brand font.

Easy Ease

06 · AFTER EFFECTS

Select keyframes and press F9. Motion speeds up and slows down naturally instead of moving like a robot.

TRY THISAnimate your logo sliding in, then apply Easy Ease and compare.
