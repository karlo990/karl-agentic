FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

What is an AI agent?

Software that understands messages, makes decisions and takes actions, such as replying to a customer, checking your calendar and confirming a booking. A chatbot only talks. An agent does the work.

Will it replace my staff?

No. It handles the repetitive first steps, like answering the same questions and collecting details, so your team spends time on guests and sales instead of typing.

Do I stay in control?

Yes. You approve the agent's tone, rules and answers before it goes live, and you can require a person to approve sensitive replies such as discounts or refunds.

Which channels do you support?

WhatsApp is our main channel, plus website chat, email, Instagram DMs and Google Calendar. We connect to the tools you already use where they have an API.

How long does it take?

A focused first agent, such as a booking assistant, is usually live in about two weeks after the discovery call.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different, so we quote after a free demo. You get a fixed setup price and a monthly running fee, with no surprises.
