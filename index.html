FAQ

Questions clients ask us.

What does Karl Agentic do?

We're a tech consultancy and creative studio. We build AI agents and bots, automate social media and admin, install and configure servers, source GPUs and hardware, and produce renders, video and design.

Do you sell NVIDIA hardware?

We source NVIDIA GPUs, workstations and servers from suppliers in South Africa, compare prices for you, then deliver, install and configure them. We are an independent consultancy, not an NVIDIA partner or reseller of record.

What about warranty and delivery?

Hardware comes with the supplier's or manufacturer's warranty. We confirm warranty, delivery time and price in writing with every quote, because stock changes quickly.

Can you host AI privately, without sending data to the cloud?

Yes. We can set up local models on your own GPU machine or server, so documents and customer data stay on your network.

Do I need hardware to use AI agents?

No. Most agents and automations run in the cloud. Hardware makes sense for private AI, heavy rendering or high volumes. We'll tell you honestly which fits.
